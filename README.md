Welcome to **TripDomain**, your all-in-one web platform designed to streamline travel planning and bookings. Stop hopping between dozens of open browser tabs—compare prices, search worldwide routes, and secure your entire vacation itinerary from a single, responsive dashboard.

---

## 🗺️ Platform Features

This hub aggregates premium travel APIs and interactive search engines across 5 major categories:

*   **✈️ Flights & Hotels:** Integrated search forms powered by Aviasales and Kiwi to discover multi-city flight paths and flight+hotel vacation packages.
*   **🏨 Accommodations:** High-converting showcase modules highlighting luxury and budget stays across major global travel hubs (London, Paris, New York, Dubai).
*   **🏛️ Tours & Experiences:** Live activity search engines from GetYourGuide alongside dynamic ticketing filters for major stadium events and local football matches.
*   **🚗 Car Hires & Transfers:** Instant pricing engines for private airport transfers and global rental car fleets via RentalCars.com and Discover Cars.
*   **🛡️ eSIM Data & Protection:** International data roaming solution setups via eSIM packs, paired with EKTA travel insurance and flight compensation assistance.

---

## 🛠️ Technology Stack & Deployment

*   **Frontend:** Semantic HTML5, CSS3 Grid/Flexbox, and asynchronous vanilla JavaScript for zero-dependency tab switching.
*   **Performance Optimization:** Asynchronous asset loading (`async`) and lazy-rendering tab states to maximize mobile page speed.
*   **Hosting:** Deployed with high availability via GitHub Pages.
*   **SEO Integration:** Fully mapped with standard XML sitemaps and verified via Google Search Console.
